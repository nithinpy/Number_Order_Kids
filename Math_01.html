Host on Your Laptop for Home Wi-Fi Access

Access this app & your local Ollama AI from any laptop, iPad, or phone at home!

Step 1: Host this HTML file using Node.js (Zero install needed!)

Save this page as index.html in a folder on your laptop, open your terminal in that folder, and run:

npx http-server -p 8080 -a 0.0.0.0 --cors

This instantly serves the app across your entire home Wi-Fi network on port 8080.

Step 2: Allow Ollama to accept Wi-Fi & Browser Connections

By default, Ollama only listens to 127.0.0.1. To let other laptops/tablets on your Wi-Fi use your laptop's Ollama AI, set these two environment variables before running Ollama:

On Windows (PowerShell):

$env:OLLAMA_HOST="0.0.0.0"; $env:OLLAMA_ORIGINS="*"; ollama serve

On Mac / Linux (Terminal):

OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0 OLLAMA_ORIGINS="*" ollama serve

Step 3: Open on your Daughter's Laptop or Tablet!

Find your host laptop's Wi-Fi IP address (e.g., 192.168.1.50 via ipconfig or ifconfig) and open http://192.168.1.50:8080 on any device at home. The app automatically detects your laptop's IP for Ollama!
